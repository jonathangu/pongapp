## Translation to game assets

Preserve four-lobed open deck, a central crossing, teal/ivory/brass materials and two distinct crew. Lower walls/enlarge characters for phone legibility. Creature mesh parts are separately articulated, with bounded triangle/material budgets: shell+claws, manta wings/lure, bell+tentacles, leviathan head/segments. Concept micro-detail is simplified deliberately for real-time mobile rendering.
